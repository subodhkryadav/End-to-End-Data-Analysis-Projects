# Healthcare Operations & Cost Optimization Analytics

## Business Scenario

A multi-specialty hospital wants to improve its operational efficiency
by analyzing patient flow, waiting time, department utilization,
treatment costs and historical patient demand.

The hospital currently has operational data across multiple areas,
but management lacks a centralized analytical view of performance.

## Business Problem

The hospital wants to identify operational bottlenecks,
understand factors affecting patient waiting time and treatment cost,
monitor department performance, and forecast future patient demand
to support better resource planning.

## Business Objective

1. Analyze patient flow across hospital departments.
2. Identify departments and time periods with high waiting times.
3. Measure resource and department utilization.
4. Analyze treatment and operational costs.
5. Identify patterns affecting patient experience.
6. Forecast future patient demand.
7. Build an interactive Power BI dashboard for management.
