# Grocery Demand, Customer & Product Intelligence

## 📌 Project Overview

This project is an end-to-end data analytics and machine learning project focused on understanding grocery purchasing behavior, customer retention, product demand, and product relationships.

The project uses a large-scale real-world grocery order dataset and combines data analytics, data warehousing, business intelligence, and machine learning to answer practical business questions.

The goal is to transform raw transactional data into actionable insights that can support:

- Demand planning
- Customer retention
- Product strategy
- Cross-selling opportunities
- Reorder prediction
- Customer segmentation

---

## 🎯 Business Problem

A large grocery platform generates millions of customer orders and product-level transactions.

The key challenge is to understand:

1. How customers purchase products over time.
2. Which products and departments drive repeated purchases.
3. What factors influence customer reordering behavior.
4. Which products are frequently purchased together.
5. How customers can be segmented based on purchasing behavior.
6. Whether machine learning can help predict future product reorders.

---

## 🔍 Key Business Questions

- What are the most frequently purchased products?
- Which products have the highest reorder rates?
- When do customers place the most orders?
- How does purchasing behavior vary across departments?
- Which products are frequently purchased together?
- What customer segments can be identified from purchasing behavior?
- Can we predict whether a customer will reorder a product?
- Which products have strong cross-selling potential?

## 📊 Dataset

The project uses the **Instacart Market Basket Analysis** dataset.

The dataset contains millions of grocery orders and tens of millions of product-level order records along with product, aisle, department and customer-order information.

Dataset source:

**Kaggle – Instacart Market Basket Analysis**
